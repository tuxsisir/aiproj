The Reality of Bylaw Enforcement

Why 70% of Strata Fines Get Overturned at the Civil Resolution Tribunal (CRT)

When an owner disputes a fine, the tribunal does not care about what council members remember in private emails. The tribunal demands an airtight, chronological evidentiary paper trail.

1. Deemed Service Failures

The Problem: Sending an email from a manager's inbox lacks certified delivery logs. Councils routinely count 14 days without factoring in the mandatory 4-day statutory deemed delivery buffer.

CRT Consequence: The tribunal declares the notice defective and wipes out the fine entirely.

2. Missing Quorum & Votes

The Problem: A property manager debits $200 onto a unit ledger without a recorded, formal council majority vote logged after the response deadline expired.

CRT Consequence: Adjudicators strike down the penalty for procedural unfairness.

3. Unsearchable Inboxes

The Problem: When a dispute escalates 9 months later, the original property manager has changed jobs, and critical emails, CCTV exports, and hearing notes are gone forever.

CRT Consequence: Council spends 20+ unpaid hours scrambling for lost files and loses by default.
