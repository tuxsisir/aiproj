03 · How it works

Set up your building in an afternoon.

No new habits required. Keep using email; CivicDesk turns it into a record.

  1. 1

    Claim your building

    Sign up, choose your strata plan and role. We approve every member, so only your council and manager get in.

  2. 2

    Upload, then forward

    Drop in your bylaws and minutes. Forward the email threads that matter to your building's CivicDesk address.

  3. 3

    Decide together

    Council and manager see the same summaries, deadlines and votes, and everyone is notified when something needs them.
